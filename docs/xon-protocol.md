# XON Protocol — Seven Strategic Practices

The XON Protocol consists of seven repeatable practices to translate values into structures and activities that endure.

1. Clarify Purpose — Facilitate a shared articulation of core purpose and mission.
2. Map Roles & Relationships — Create a living organogram of roles, authorities, and succession lines.
3. Codify Principles — Translate non-negotiables into policies and decision trees.
4. Encode Rituals & Practices — Institutionalize rituals for transmission of values and knowledge.
5. Preserve Provenance — Maintain metadata and archives for decisions, documents, and teachings.
6. Iterate Governance — Schedule regular reviews, sunset clauses, and adaptive mechanisms.
7. Resource Perpetuity — Establish financial, human, and institutional mechanisms to sustain the mission.

Each protocol entry should include:
- Purpose & outcome
- Steps to implement
- Templates and artifacts
- Measurement and review cadence
