# Ten Non-Negotiables

This document captures the core ethical and operational principles that guide Dynasty Legacy. Each non-negotiable should be documented with rationale, practical implications, and examples.

1. Stewardship of Purpose — Commit to a clearly articulated purpose that guides decisions across generations.
2. Truthful Narrative — Preserve honest histories and transparently document decisions and their rationales.
3. Shared Accountability — Define roles, responsibilities, and accountability mechanisms across lineage and organization.
4. Longevity over Short-Term Gain — Prioritize intergenerational resilience over short-term advantage.
5. Equity & Inclusion — Design policies that protect marginalized voices within the lineage or organization.
6. Ritualized Transmission — Create repeatable rituals and practices to transmit values and knowledge.
7. Adaptive Governance — Incorporate mechanisms to evolve governance as contexts change.
8. Knowledge Preservation — Systematically archive lessons, stories, and decisions with provenance metadata.
9. Ethical Use of Technology — Use AI and other technologies transparently, with clear consent and oversight.
10. Perpetual Reinvestment — Allocate surplus resources toward mission-aligned perpetuation (scholarships, institutions, trusts).

For each item above, include:
- Rationale
- Practical application
- Example scenarios
- Suggested artifacts (documents, templates, rituals)
